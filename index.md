# Why I'm Learning Cybersecurity

Hello, I'm Mohammad Hesham. I am an Aeronautical Engineering graduate with a specialization in Avionics, and I have always been deeply fascinated by cybersecurity. I believe that a strong foundation in cyber hygiene and web security is crucial, regardless of one's career path. For me, this knowledge is especially vital as it directly complements my long-term goals and specialization within the aerospace and avionics sector.

I am beginning this journey with a dedicated focus on web application security. My objective is to build a methodical approach to security testing—understanding how systems break and, more importantly, how to defend them. To achieve this, I am utilizing platforms like the PortSwigger Web Security Academy to gain hands-on, practical experience.

This website will serve as my public portfolio. It is where I will publish my lab walkthroughs, technical notes, and progress. I am starting from the ground up with 0% completion, but this platform is my commitment to showing up every day, doing the work, and documenting the journey.
